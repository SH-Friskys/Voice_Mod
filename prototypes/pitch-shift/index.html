Pitch shift listening test PROTOTYPE

Record your voice once, then hear it through each pitch shifter. Budget from “Set the latency budget”: pitch shift may use about 60 ms before the 120 ms ceiling is broken; about 20 ms would also hit the 80 ms target.

1 · Record your voice

Speak naturally for 10–15 seconds, the way you would in a dub or a call. Mic processing (echo cancel, auto-gain, noise suppression) is switched off so you hear the raw shifter.

2 · Voice settings (apply to every option)

Quick picks:

Tone/formant and “keep natural” only affect Signalsmith Stretch; the delay-line shifter can’t do them.
